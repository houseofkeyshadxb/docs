## CANVA DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

**Primary size:** 2000 x 2000px (Etsy product image — square)

## DESIGN CONCEPT: "Open Book / Organized Library"

**Visual Theme:** Clean, organized, professional. Like a well-curated bookshelf meets a tech dashboard. Conveys: organized knowledge, practical value, instant usability.

---

## COLOR PALETTE:
- **Background:** Deep navy to rich purple gradient (#1A1040 → #2D1B69)
- **Primary Accent:** Warm gold/amber (#F59E0B) — premium feel
- **Secondary:** Clean white (#FFFFFF)
- **Claude brand reference:** Subtle orange-copper tones (#C96B2C) — nod to Anthropic/Claude
- **Card backgrounds:** Slightly lighter purple with subtle border

## ALTERNATIVE CONCEPT: "Code/Terminal Aesthetic"
If the card concept feels too flat, try this instead:

- Bright cursor blinking effect
- Category labels appear as "file tabs" at the top
- More techy, less polished — good for developer audience

- Main number "500": **Bebas Neue** (massive, fills space)
- "PROMPTS": **Inter Bold** or **Space Grotesk Bold**  
- Category names on cards: **DM Sans Medium**
- "for Business Owners": **Inter Regular** in white
