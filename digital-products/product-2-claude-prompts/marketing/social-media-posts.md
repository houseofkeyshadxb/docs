Most Claude AI users get mediocre results because they write mediocre prompts.

**Tweet 2/7:**
Instead of:
"Write me an email to my client"

Write:
"Write a 150-word email to [CLIENT] about [TOPIC]. Tone: [VOICE]. Include: [KEY POINTS]. CTA: [ACTION]. Do NOT: [WHAT TO AVOID]."

Specificity = better output. Every time.

---

**Tweet 3/7:**
Instead of:
"Give me marketing ideas"

Write:
"Give me 10 marketing ideas for [BUSINESS TYPE] targeting [AUDIENCE] with a budget of $[AMOUNT]. Rank by ease + impact. Include one contrarian idea."

---

**Tweet 4/7:**
Instead of:
"Write a sales page"

Write:
"Write a complete sales page for [PRODUCT] at $[PRICE]. Include: problem section, solution, 5 benefits, 3 objection responses, FAQ, and 2 CTA variations. Target: [BUYER]."

---

**Tweet 5/7:**
Instead of:
"Help me with my business"

Write:
"I run [BUSINESS]. My revenue goal is $[AMOUNT]. My bottleneck is [PROBLEM]. Give me 5 specific, actionable steps to fix this. Rank by impact."

---

**Tweet 6/7:**
The pattern:
• Be specific about WHO it's for
• Be specific about FORMAT you want
• Be specific about CONSTRAINTS
• Tell it what to AVOID
• Ask for MULTIPLE options
