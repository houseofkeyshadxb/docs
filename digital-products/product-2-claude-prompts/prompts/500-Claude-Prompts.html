📧 Email & Communication

Client emails, cold outreach, proposals, follow-ups, team communication, and customer service

Cold Outreach Email
#14
Write a cold outreach email to [PROSPECT NAME] at [COMPANY]. Their likely pain: [PROBLEM THEY PROBABLY HAVE]. My solution: [WHAT I OFFER]. Proof: [ONE CLIENT RESULT]. CTA: Low-commitment (15-min call, not "buy now"). Subject line: 3 options, each under 50 characters. Keep body under 100 words — respects their time.
💡 Best model: claude-sonnet-4-6
Proposal Email
#7
Write a proposal email for [PROSPECT NAME] at [COMPANY]. Their problem: [PROBLEM]. My solution: [SOLUTION]. Investment: $[PRICE RANGE]. Timeline: [TIMEFRAME]. Include: 3 specific outcomes they'll get. Attach note: "Full proposal attached". CTA: Schedule 30-min call to review.
Difficult Client Response
#3
Write a professional response to this difficult client email: [PASTE CLIENT EMAIL] My position: [YOUR RESPONSE/STANCE]. Goal: Maintain the relationship while setting clear boundaries. Tone: Firm but empathetic. Do not: Apologize excessively or make promises I can't keep.
💡 Best model: claude-opus-4-7 for sensitive situations
Payment Request (Polite)
#4
Write a polite payment reminder email to [CLIENT NAME]. Invoice #: [NUMBER] Amount: $[AMOUNT] Due date: [DATE — now overdue by X days] Keep it: Professional, not aggressive, preserve the relationship. Include: Invoice details and payment method/link.
Cancellation Response (Save the Client)
#9
A client just sent me this cancellation email: [PASTE THEIR EMAIL] My goal: Save the relationship and understand their reason. Write a response that: - Acknowledges their decision without panic - Asks one question to understand their reason - Mentions one thing that might address their concern - Leaves door open to continue Do not: Beg, offer discounts immediately, or be desperate.
Testimonial Request
#10
Write an email requesting a testimonial from happy client [CLIENT NAME]. Project/service delivered: [WHAT YOU DID FOR THEM]. Result they got: [SPECIFIC RESULT IF KNOWN]. Where to leave review: [Google/Trustpilot/LinkedIn/your website]. Keep it: Short (under 150 words), easy to act on. Offer: [Optional — offer something small in return].

Length: Comprehensive — don't skip any section.

#83
