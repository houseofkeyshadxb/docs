# THUMBNAIL DESIGN BRIEF
## Product 3: "WhatsApp Business Automation Blueprint"

---

## CANVA DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

**Primary size:** 1280 x 720px (Gumroad standard)
**Also create:** 1080x1080 (Instagram), 1200x628 (LinkedIn/Facebook preview)

---

## DESIGN CONCEPT: "WhatsApp on Steroids"

**Visual Theme:** Recognizable WhatsApp green + professional dark overlay. 
Instantly signals: "This is about WhatsApp" + "This is serious/professional"

---

## COLOR PALETTE:
- **Background:** Dark charcoal/near-black (#1A1A2E or #0D1117)
- **Primary Accent:** WhatsApp green (#25D366) — use this prominently
- **Secondary:** White (#FFFFFF)
- **Highlight:** Gold/amber (#F59E0B) for price and key callouts
- **UAE/MENA nod:** Optional: small UAE flag or Arabic pattern element

---

## UAE/MENA SPECIFIC ELEMENTS:
- Consider adding small UAE flag or Burj Khalifa silhouette icon
- OR Arabic calligraphy element (subtle, not overdone)
- OR "مرحبا! 👋" (Hello in Arabic) in the chat mockup

---

## FONT RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Main headline: **Montserrat Black** or **Bebas Neue** (massive, bold)
- Sub-text: **Inter Medium** or **DM Sans**
- Chat mockup: **SF Pro** or **Roboto** (system font look)
- Price badge: **Oswald Bold**

---

## WHAT TO AVOID:
- ❌ WhatsApp's actual logo (trademark issues) — use a phone icon instead
- ❌ Overly complex chat mockup (keep it simple)
- ❌ Neon colors — keep the green in the WhatsApp shade
- ❌ Clip art-style phone icons — use clean, minimal phone frame

---
