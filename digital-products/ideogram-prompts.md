## TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS ON IDEOGRAM:

1. **Run 4 variations** — Ideogram generates 4 at once, pick the best
2. **Remix** — If one is 80% right, click "Remix" and refine the prompt
3. **Add "no watermarks, no logos"** if you get unwanted elements
4. **Text accuracy** — Ideogram is the BEST AI for readable text in images
5. **After generating** — Download PNG, then add exact price/text overlay in Canva as text layer for perfect accuracy
