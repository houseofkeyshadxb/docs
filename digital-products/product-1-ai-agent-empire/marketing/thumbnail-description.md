# THUMBNAIL DESIGN BRIEF
## Product 1: "The AI Agent Empire"

---

## CANVA DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

**Canvas Size:** 1280 x 720px (Gumroad standard product image)
**Also create:** 1:1 version (1080x1080) for Instagram/social media

---

## DESIGN CONCEPT: "Command Center"

**Visual Theme:** Dark tech aesthetic — like a mission control dashboard. 
Conveys power, automation, and professional systems.

---

## COLOR PALETTE:
- **Background:** Deep dark navy/black (#0A0E1A)
- **Primary Accent:** Electric blue (#00D4FF) — glow effects
- **Secondary Accent:** Purple/violet (#7B2FBE)
- **Text:** Pure white (#FFFFFF) and light blue (#B3E5FC)
- **Highlight boxes:** Semi-transparent dark with blue border

---

## LAYOUT STRUCTURE:

### TOP SECTION (25% of height):
```
🤖 THE AI AGENT EMPIRE
```
- Font: Bold, futuristic (Orbitron or Space Grotesk Bold)
- Size: Large, spanning full width
- Color: White with electric blue glow effect
- Add subtle scan-line texture behind text

### MIDDLE SECTION (50% of height):
6 agent icons arranged in 2 rows of 3, each in a glowing hexagon:
```
[📧 Inbox]  [✍️ Content]  [🔍 Scout]
[💰 Leads]  [💻 Code]    [📊 Analytics]
```
- Each hexagon: Dark background, colored icon, small label below
- Connecting lines between hexagons (like a network/circuit)
- One hexagon slightly larger or brighter (showing active state)

### BOTTOM SECTION (25% of height):
Left side:
```
Setup 6 Autonomous Agents
in 48 Hours
```
(Font: Clean sans-serif, white)

Right side:
```
$97
```
(Font: Very large, bold, electric blue — make price feel like a deal)

---

## ADDITIONAL DESIGN ELEMENTS:
- **Background texture:** Subtle grid/circuit board pattern at low opacity
- **Particles/dots:** Small glowing dots scattered (like stars or data points)
- **Glow effects:** Blue glow on all hexagons and borders
- **Small badge:** "Scripts Included" or "Python + AI" badge in corner

---

## TEXT HIERARCHY:
1. "THE AI AGENT EMPIRE" — biggest, most prominent
2. "6 Autonomous Agents" — second biggest
3. "48 Hours" — highlighted in accent color
4. Agent names in hexagons — small but readable

---

## WHAT TO AVOID:
- ❌ Clipart-looking robots
- ❌ Stock photo of person at laptop
- ❌ Light/white backgrounds (this should look premium/dark)
- ❌ Too many elements — keep it clean despite dark theme
- ❌ Comic Sans or casual fonts

---

## CANVA FONT RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Headlines: **Space Grotesk Bold** or **Orbitron Bold**
- Body/labels: **Inter Medium** or **DM Sans**
- Price: **Bebas Neue** (large, impactful)
